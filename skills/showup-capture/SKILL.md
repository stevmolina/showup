# Show Up capture

Leave a useful record of what happened without turning content into another task list.

Read [work sources and safety](../../references/sources.md) before inspecting connected apps or writing to Notion.

## Workflow

1. Start with the material Steven supplied. Inspect GitHub, Linear, Codex, Notion, or Calendar only when the request calls for it or the supplied evidence points there.
2. Separate meaningful moments from mechanical activity. Preserve exact links, dates, tool names, numbers, decisions, and outcomes.
3. Record the human gap as a short question when the evidence cannot reveal why the moment mattered. Do not invent feelings or intent.
4. Classify the item as raw material, a promising seed, shipped proof, sensitive pattern, or not worth keeping.
5. When the request includes capture, create or update the Notion Content Inbox entry using its live schema.

For each kept moment, store what happened, why someone in tech might care, the tension or surprise, the evidence URL, project, date, shareability, and missing context.

## Boundaries

- Capture source material. Draft copy only when Steven also asks for a draft.
- Keep private work private. A generalized pattern must contain no identifying client information.
- Ordinary activity does not create Todoist tasks, recurring reminders, or artificial deadlines.

## Done

The capture is complete when each saved item has traceable evidence, a clear reason it may matter, a safety classification, and a verified Notion page or row when a write was requested.
