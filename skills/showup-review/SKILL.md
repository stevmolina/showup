# Show Up review

Use outcomes to improve the system instead of rewarding activity.

Read [Steven's social voice](../../references/voice.md) and [work sources and safety](../../references/sources.md).

## Workflow

1. Inspect the requested period in Notion and the connected publishing platform. Use live data rather than remembered counts.
2. Count captured seeds, completed drafts, and published posts separately.
3. Identify the strongest proof and the posts that earned meaningful responses. Distinguish useful signal from raw impressions.
4. Find stale, duplicated, weak, or unsafe ideas. Recommend archiving them rather than carrying an endless backlog.
5. Compare Steven's approved edits and successful posts with the voice reference. Propose a voice update only when the evidence repeats or Steven explicitly asks for one.
6. Choose one next story based on proof, human relevance, freshness, and shareability.

Write review results or voice changes back to Notion or the plugin only when Steven requests persistence.

## Done

The review is complete when it reports verified output counts, the strongest evidence-backed lesson, material to archive, any supported voice change, and one next story to develop.
